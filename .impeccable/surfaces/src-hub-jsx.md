# Game Night: every surface

Scope: the whole client: home hub, join, each game's setup, TV display, phone, host controller and end screens. Mode: Experience on the TV and home hub (the show leads), Operate on phones, setup and the controller (one obvious action, precise details).

Audience and job: adult friends at a party; the host sets up on a shared screen (TV, laptop or projector, often dim), players act on their phones.

Memorable moment: the TV as a lit backglass whose dot-matrix display counts the clock down and flashes scores and verdicts.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Game Night is a pinball cabinet. The shared screen is the backglass, each phone is the lockdown bar with its flipper buttons, each game is its own table. Refuses the category default of flat rounded party cards on a purple ground.

OWN-WORLD: Cabinet black-violet ground (#0b0614 family); airbrushed backglass fields in each game's colours (Jeopardy blue/gold, Survey orange, Spin & Solve magenta, Fib mint) over a shared magenta-violet house art; chrome-bevelled trim and chrome display lettering (Archivo Black); an orange plasma dot-matrix display (a dotted monospace on a dark grille) for every score, clock, code and verdict, and nowhere else; lit playfield inserts (rounded arrow and circle lamps) for choices; big domed flipper-style buttons on phones. States are lamp states: off, lit, flashing, locked. Raises: every state distinct without colour (tensegrity); dot-matrix sizes in whole dots (azulejo); lock-in latches one-way (ebru); team games mirror across the centre line (labanotation).

STORY: The host sees four tables and picks one; players key a code into the dot-matrix and join; the TV plays the show and the DMD calls the moments.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home at 1440x900: backglass panel fills the top ~75% inside a chrome bezel, GAME NIGHT in chrome lettering upper third, tagline below, four game inserts across the lower part of the glass each with name, player count and a set-up action; under the glass, the DMD strip holds the join-code entry and Join button as the primary player action.
